Guttas Førstevalg : twenty-plus years of "we probably have one of those."

Arngren Sky Commuter AS has run arngren.net out of Oslo since 2002, run by founder Frithjof Arngren. The catalog spans electric vehicles, robots and RC gear, consumer electronics, and outdoor hobby equipment. This is an independent redesign concept, not the operating business.

2002

Arngren Sky Commuter AS opens

Founded by Frithjof Arngren in Oslo, starting with consumer electronics and gradually adding electric vehicles, robots, and outdoor equipment as the catalog grew.

2004

The site takes shape

The original arngren.net went live in its now-famous form, every item, every photo, on a single page with no navigation to speak of. It became a well-known example of maximalist web design.

2026

This redesign

A concept exploring what the same catalog looks like with structure: categories, search, a product page per item, and a lot more whitespace using the real product lineup and the real "Guttas Førstevalg!" badge colors.
